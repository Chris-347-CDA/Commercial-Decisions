Where's the margin opportunity in your menu?

Two quick calculations. The first shows your current margin on one drink. The second shows three different ways to grow it, side by side — each one within your control.

Worth saying up front: this isn't a set of instructions, and it isn't trying to give you an answer. It won't tell you "raise your price by 5% and you'll make £X." It's about asking a more logical question first — how big is the prize, actually — so you can judge whether any of this is worth pursuing at all, and if so, which lever's worth going after first.

This is one drink.

Different items on your menu carry different amounts of headroom on each of these three levers — and for multi-site owners, the answer often looks different at every location. If any of these numbers came out small, that's a genuinely useful answer too: it means it's not worth chasing right now. If they didn't, the next question is what actually fixing the root cause — the right tools, training, or process — would take, and whether it's worth it.

  • Price and speed use your actual numbers; waste uses an industry benchmark, not a measurement
  • The combined figure assumes all three are achieved together — in practice this usually happens one step at a time
  • It's one item — a full menu review usually surfaces two or three with far more room than you'd expect

If any of this was useful, it's worth talking through what it could look like across your whole menu.
